\section{Technology Selection Comparisons}
\label{appendix:techstack}

Section~\ref{sec:tech_stack} states each technology decision and the constraint that drove it. The comparison matrices behind those decisions are collected here: one per component, scoring the option chosen against the alternatives that were seriously considered. They are reference material for a reader checking the reasoning rather than following it, which is why they sit apart from the argument they support.
