\section{Lighthouse Audit Reports}
\label{appendix:lighthouse}

This appendix reproduces the full Google Lighthouse audit evidence for the five pages summarised in Table~\ref{tab:lighthouse_results}. Each audit ran against the production deployment (\path|https://abridgeai.tech|) with desktop emulation, Lighthouse~v13 on Chrome~153. Authenticated pages were audited through a real signed-in session, so the rendered views below show the authenticated application rather than a login redirect.

For each page, two figures are given: the Lighthouse category scores with the core web-vital metrics, and the final rendered screenshot captured by the audit itself.
