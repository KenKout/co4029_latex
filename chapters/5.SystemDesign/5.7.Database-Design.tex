\subsection{Database Design}
\label{sec:database-design}

This section presents the Entity-Relationship Diagram of the main database --- the structure every feature reads and writes. The logical and physical designs derived from it, which restate the same structure at the level of relations and of storage, are given in Appendix~\ref{appendix:database-design}.

The schema is presented first as an overall map, then through focused diagrams for the quiz, interview, and remaining cross-domain entities. Splitting the ERD makes the primary keys, foreign keys, and domain-specific relationships readable without losing the overall context.
