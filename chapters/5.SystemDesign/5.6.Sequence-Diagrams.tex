\subsection{Sequence Diagrams}
\label{sec:sequence-diagrams}

Sequence diagrams illustrate the time-based interactions
between system components, including the client
application, backend services, AI engines,
and database infrastructure.

The sequence diagrams focus on the core workflows
of the aBridgeAI platform, including authentication,
smart content ingestion, and Human-in-the-Loop quiz
generation. These diagrams emphasize asynchronous processing,
AI integration, and communication across the hybrid
system architecture.

Reproducing a representative subset here would duplicate the appendix without
adding to the argument, since each diagram is only legible at full width and
each carries the same weight as the others. The retained rendered diagrams ---
authentication, smart content ingestion, and Human-in-the-Loop quiz generation ---
are presented in Appendix~\ref{appendix:sequence-diagrams}. The mock-interview
raster sequence was replaced with a code-backed native textual trace in that
appendix because its former routed/Qdrant narrative no longer represents the
deployed runtime; Section~\ref{sec:impl_interviews} provides the full implementation detail.
