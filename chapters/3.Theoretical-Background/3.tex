\section{Theoretical Background}

\textit{To address the structural deficiencies and semantic isolation of traditional educational systems identified in Chapter 2, aBridgeAI relies on a rigorous synthesis of cognitive psychology, algorithmic modeling, and advanced artificial intelligence. This chapter establishes the theoretical and technological foundations that empower the system to treat knowledge as a dynamic, interdependent cognitive ecosystem.}

\textit{First, the chapter explores the baseline constraints of human memory through CLT, establishing the pedagogical necessity for adaptive knowledge structuring. These psychological principles are then translated into computational mechanics through the design of a hybrid SM-2 SR algorithm and individualized learner evaluation metrics. Finally, the chapter examines the sophisticated technological infrastructure required to execute these models at scale—specifically LLMs, RAG pipelines, Vector Databases, and modern web development frameworks. Together, these theoretical pillars form the architectural blueprint for aBridgeAI's context-aware learning environment.}
