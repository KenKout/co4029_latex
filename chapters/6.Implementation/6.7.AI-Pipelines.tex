\subsection{AI Generation Pipelines}
\label{sec:impl_ai_pipelines}

AI-assisted generation creates teacher-reviewed drafts from course materials. The system first retrieves relevant material that the requesting user is authorised to access. It then uses this context to produce draft assessment content and performs automated consistency checks before saving the results for review.

\subsubsection{Quiz Generation}

Quiz generation follows four stages:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Retrieve context} --- select relevant authorised course material.
    \item \textbf{Generate drafts} --- create multiple-choice questions, answer options, and explanations grounded in that material.
    \item \textbf{Check drafts} --- remove unsuitable or duplicate items where possible.
    \item \textbf{Save for review} --- store the remaining items as drafts for the teacher.
\end{enumerate}

Generated quiz questions are not published automatically. The teacher may approve, edit, or reject each draft before learners can access it.
