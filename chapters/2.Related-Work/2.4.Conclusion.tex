\subsection{The Path Toward aBridgeAI}

The limitations outlined above—semantic isolation in SRS, insufficient experimental control in retrieval practice implementations, relational blindness in RAG, and the maintenance fragility of large-scale Knowledge Graph Construction (KGC)—converge toward a singular conclusion. Contemporary systems continue to treat knowledge as a collection of isolated data points rather than as a dynamic, interdependent cognitive ecosystem.

By integrating context-aware algorithms with rigorous dependency management and cognitively grounded retrieval strategies, \textit{aBridgeAI} directly addresses these structural deficiencies. The system advances beyond simple recall optimization toward deep structural understanding, offering a unified and adaptive framework capable of supporting both individual learners and enterprise-scale knowledge infrastructures.
