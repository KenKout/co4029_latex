\section*{About this volume}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{About this volume}

This is the second of two volumes. Volume~I carries the thesis itself --- the
motivation, the related work, the analysis, the design, the implementation and
the evaluation --- together with the appendices a reader needs \emph{while}
reading it: the technology comparisons, the algorithm and process-flow
specifications, the database design, and the activity, sequence and class
diagrams.

This volume carries the reference catalogues. They are consulted rather than
read: the full requirements specifications, every use case, the complete API
surface, the site map, the user-interface inventory, the test-case
specifications, and the Lighthouse audit reports. Splitting them out keeps
Volume~I at a length that can be read end to end, without removing anything
from the record --- every table that was in the single-volume edition is here,
unchanged.

Cross-references work in both directions. A reference in Volume~I to an
appendix here resolves to its letter in this volume, and a reference here to a
section of Volume~I resolves to that section's number.
